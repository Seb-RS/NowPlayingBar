## Added
- **Vertical taskbar support** with a compact layout. The bar can be dragged up and down.
- Scrolling title and artist on vertical taskbars, so long names are readable.
- **Start with Windows** toggle in the right-click menu (on by default).

## Changed
- Previous and next icons are now centered in their buttons.

## Fixed
- Opening the app twice no longer creates two bars (single instance).
- Players that are closed and reopened are detected again.
- Cover images are now released from memory when the song changes.
- In **All screens** mode, connecting or disconnecting a monitor now adds or removes its bar.
- Opening the right-click menu no longer shows the app icon in the taskbar.
