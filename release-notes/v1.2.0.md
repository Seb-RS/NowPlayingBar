## Added
- **Player priority.** When several players are open, the one that is playing wins, and the bar doesn't jump away while it keeps playing. Among several playing at once, the one that started most recently is shown.
- **Source** menu (right-click → Source): keep it on **Automatic** or always show one specific app.
